Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Starbucks88 collects, uses, and protects player information.

Information Collected

Starbucks88 collects information provided during registration, including contact details and account credentials. Transaction records, including deposits, withdrawals, and game activity, are retained. Technical data such as browser type, device information, and IP address may be logged for security and operational purposes.

Use of Information

Information is used to operate the account, process transactions, verify identity, provide support, and comply with applicable requirements. Aggregated data may be used to improve platform performance. Personal information is not sold to third parties.

Data Protection

Data transmitted between the player's browser and Starbucks88 servers is encrypted. Access to personal information is restricted to personnel who require it for operational purposes. Players are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials.

Retention

Account and transaction records are retained for the period required by operational and regulatory considerations. Players who wish to close their account may contact support. Certain records may be retained after closure where required.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to support through live chat.
